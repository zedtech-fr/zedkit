Touch-friendly controls: on touch or narrow screens, inputs, selects, textareas, buttons and tab lists reach a 44px tap height, and checkbox and switch carry an invisible 44px hit area while keeping their visible size. Mouse users keep compact heights. Dialogs keep a side gutter on phones and their close button is a full tap target, buttons get a subtle press feedback (skipped with reduced motion), invalid fields show a destructive border, wide tables fade the cut-off edge, and the data table resets to the first page when filtering and hides pagination when empty.
