Add selectable identities: `data-identity` on the root swaps radius, border width, shadows, fonts, palette and motion tokens next to light/dark. Ships six identities: `mist` (default), `press`, `tide`, `volt`, `maison` and `glass`, plus an `IdentityScript` (no flash before hydration, maps older ids), a labelled `IdentitySwitcher`, an `IdentityScope` and an `IdentityCycle` hero preview that crossfades through the identities, `Reveal` and `Stagger` wrappers, and `animate-*`, `stagger-*`, `hover-lift`, `hover-press` utilities that respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. The default look is unchanged.
