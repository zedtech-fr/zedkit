Add selectable identities: `data-identity` on the root swaps radius, border width, shadows, fonts, palette and motion tokens next to light/dark. Ships five identities: `mist` (default), `press`, `tide`, `volt` and `gilt`, plus an `IdentityScript` (no flash before hydration), a labelled `IdentitySwitcher`, `Reveal` and `Stagger` wrappers, and `animate-*`, `stagger-*`, `hover-lift`, `hover-press` utilities that respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. The default look is unchanged.
