Soft warning badges use `--warning` ink on the 15% wash so caption-size text stays 4.5:1. Stock and Mist filled warning pills are darker in light (light ink) and lighter in dark (dark ink), the same class of change as the destructive fill. Stock and Mist light `--success` and `--destructive` are a step darker (and dark `--destructive` a step lighter) so soft-success and soft-danger ink on the same wash also stay 4.5:1.
