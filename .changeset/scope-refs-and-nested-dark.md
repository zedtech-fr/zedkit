`ThemeScope` composes a consumer `ref` (object or callback, cleanup included) with its internal one, so overlays keep mounting in the scope. A scope that only pins a mode inside another scope keeps that scope's identity, and a page-root identity no longer overrides the dark section of a nearer identity. The scoped Mist reset follows the Inter family Mist is documented to use. Adds A11y guidance for SecretField, Form, Stack, Select and Checkbox/Switch.
